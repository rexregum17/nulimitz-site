AI Automation

Follow up, even when you're busy.

When someone calls, fills out a form, or finds you online, they should hear back right away. We set up simple automations for small local businesses so follow-up, booking, and reminders still happen when you're on a job.

See the full page
01

Follow-up by email and text

After someone fills out a form on your site, they get a thank you and a short follow-up over the next few days, by email and text.

02

Website assistant

A simple chat on your site that answers common questions and takes a name and number when you're not there to reply.

03

Missed-call text back

If a call comes in while you're busy, they get a text asking how you can help, so the lead doesn't walk away.

04

Review requests

After a job is done, we send a friendly ask for a Google review at the right time.

05

Booking and reminders

Let customers pick a time and get a reminder before the appointment, so fewer people forget to show up.

06

Lead tagging in your CRM

Every lead is saved and tagged with where it came from, so you can see what is working and follow up in one place.

07

Social post scheduling

Posts written and scheduled ahead of time, so your Facebook and Instagram stay active during the week.
