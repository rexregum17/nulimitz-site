A few common
questions

Short answers, and we're happy to talk through the rest.

What kinds of businesses is this for?

Local shops and service businesses, like salons, restaurants, contractors, and clinics. If you get leads by phone, form, or social media, we can probably help.

Do I need a website first?

It helps, and we can build one if you don't have one yet. Some automations, like missed-call text back and review requests, can run even without a new site.

Can this work with the tools I already use?

Often yes. Tell us what you use for email, texting, booking, or your customer list, and we'll work with it when we can, or suggest a simple setup if you don't have one.

How involved do I need to be?

We'll need a little time up front to learn how you work and to approve the messages that go out. After that, the system runs in the background, and you can jump in whenever a lead needs you.

How do I get started?

Send us a note with what you want to automate. We'll follow up with a free quote. No pressure.
